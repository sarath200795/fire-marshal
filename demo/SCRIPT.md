## Full narration (plain text)

Welcome to Fire Marshal — the app that helps you track every fire extinguisher across all your sites.

In this short tutorial, we'll show you how to register, add equipment, and monitor compliance.

First, register your organization. The first person to sign up automatically becomes the administrator.

Fill in your organization name, your details and a password, then create the organization.

You land on your safety dashboard — live KPIs and color-coded compliance, updated in real time.

Next, add an extinguisher. Leave the serial number blank to auto-assign one, and a public QR code is created on save.

Set the location and the service dates, then save.

The repository holds every extinguisher in one filterable, searchable list.

The To Be Refilled view automatically surfaces units approaching their next service date.

Print QR tags and affix them. Anyone can scan a tag to view status or report a defect, with no login needed.

Admins review and approve refill and defect requests, all in one place.

That's a quick tour of Fire Marshal. Thanks for watching — get started by registering your organization.
